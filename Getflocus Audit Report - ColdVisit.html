The short version

Flocus has sharp copy but asks cold visitors to commit before the product exists

The hero message and scrolling demo are genuinely compelling for a study-habit app, but a first-time visitor lands on a waitlist for a product that isn't live, with no pricing, no social proof, and no way to try anything — raising the bar for conversion significantly.

Fix this first · included in your preview

Zero social proof for an unproven, not-yet-live product

A cold visitor has no account history, no peer referral, and no way to try the product — without even one testimonial, waitlist count, or beta-user quote, there is no external signal that anyone else believes this is worth waiting for.

Recommended changeAdd a visible waitlist count ('X people already signed up') or 2–3 short beta-tester quotes directly above the hero CTA form.
